What we stand for

Mission

To give women permission to pause: a safe, judgment-free space for healing, relaxation and genuine bonding, where fun, wellness and self-care come first.

Vision

To build a lasting sisterhood that redefines how women connect and take a break, so every member leaves each event feeling recharged and supported.

Goals

  • Build genuine, healthy bonds
  • Make wellness part of every event
  • Make room for play and creativity
  • Open up natural networking
  • Celebrate womanhood beautifully

Meet the founder

“A good evening is never about the venue. It is about who sits beside you.”

Saara Tasnim is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Law at North South University and the founder of Soirée Club. She started the club to give women a safe, vibrant place to pause, connect and recharge.

Members can expect thoughtfully hosted evenings, a welcoming sisterhood and events shaped by the women who attend them.
