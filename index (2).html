What we stand for

Our mission

To bring people together for evenings worth remembering: well hosted, well curated and open to anyone who enjoys music, food and conversation.

Our vision

To become the most welcoming evening club in Bangladesh, where friendships, ideas and collaborations begin over a shared table.

Our goals

  • Host one signature event every month.
  • Grow to 500 active members by the end of 2027.
  • Give local artists, musicians and speakers a stage.
  • Hold at least two charity evenings every year.

Meet the founder

“A good evening is never about the venue. It is about who sits beside you.”

Saara Tasnim is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Law at North South University and the founder of Soirée Club. She started the club to give people in the city a place to meet, share ideas and enjoy live culture without the noise of a crowded night out.

Members can expect thoughtfully hosted evenings, a welcoming community and events shaped by the people who attend them.
